# Veille AO Afrique

Open public tenders in Morocco, Benin and Tunisia (national portals) and French-language World Bank notices for francophone Africa, refreshed twice a day and published as a static French site with filters, one page per tender, a rule-based summary (no LLM), an e-mail alerts interest form and a paid-tier fake door.

**Site:** https://ao-afrique.jalonel.com/ (since 2026-10-16, served at the root of its Jalonel subdomain)
A service of READ FRANCE SAS (SIREN 988 741 773, Paris), under the brand Jalonel (« Jalonel est une marque de READ FRANCE SAS. », footer « Une marque Jalonel » → https://jalonel.com/, JSON-LD publisher Jalonel). Venture AF-XX-04 of Octave's global portfolio (`VREAD-corp/octave-global-ventures`). Independent from the Vread app.

## Site

- `/` list with client-side filters (keyword, sector, region, city/province, buyer, deadline, category, country, sort) over `data/ouverts.json`; first 30 cards server-rendered.
- `/ao/<id>-<slug>/` one page per tender: verbatim object, fact table, rule-based summary, official link, JSON-LD (`Demand`, `BreadcrumbList`). Closed tenders stay 30 days.
- `/secteurs/<slug>/` (13), `/regions/<slug>/` (12), `/afrique-francophone/` (World Bank).
- `/pays/` and `/pays/<country>/` for every country with open notices outside the Moroccan portal (Bénin and Tunisie from their national portals, the others from the World Bank), `/pays/<country>/<sector>/` per sector with an open notice. Indexed and in the sitemap from 3 open notices (`COUNTRY_MIN`), otherwise `noindex, follow`. JSON-LD `BreadcrumbList` + `CollectionPage`/`ItemList` of the tender pages (no rating or review). Linked from the nav, the home page, `/afrique-francophone/`, each sector page (« Ce secteur par pays ») and each tender page. Tender pages of the new sources use the same template, so AOAF-ALERT-INLINE-01 (`xp.js`, `#alerte-rapide`) applies unchanged; their alert forms send the country (`countries=benin|tunisie`) and the contexts `fiche-pays` / `fiche-pays-rapide`.
- Short alert sign-up (`static/alerte-inline.js`, form `#alerte-inline`: sector, region, e-mail, consent) on every tender page after the fact table, on the list (follows the sector and region filters), on each sector and region page after the first 12 cards and on `/afrique-francophone/`. Same `signup` as `/alertes/` (kind `alertes`, no plan), honest status and planned price next to the button. Measured by `alert_form_view` (detail `fiche`, `fiche-bm`, `liste`, `secteur`, `region`, `afrique`; sent once when 40 % of the box is on screen), the server-side `alert_signup` (Stats row keyed by page, `/ao/*` for tender pages), cro.js `form alerte-inline start/submit/abandon` and goal `signup`. Tender pages end with « Avis similaires encore ouverts » (same sector and region first), links to the sector and region pages and « Et maintenant ? » (official notice, deadline, alert).
- `/alertes/` interest form + « Pré-inscription, sans paiement » for two planned tiers (Essentiel 39 € and Pro 79 € for 3 months, marked « bientôt disponible »). No payment code.
- `/licence.html` « Conditions de réutilisation des données » (law 31-13 art. 6 for the Moroccan notices, CC BY 4.0 for the World Bank ones), target of the `Dataset` JSON-LD `license` property.
- `/methode/`, `/mentions-legales/`, `/confidentialite/`, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `feed.xml`, `CNAME` (`ao-afrique.jalonel.com`), IndexNow key file (`5be19f56d46ca3d2553bee28ebfa8edd.txt`, the key shared by the Jalonel sites).

## Backend (Apps Script, `gas/`)

A new standalone project `veille-ao-afrique-gas (READ FRANCE SAS)`, scope `spreadsheets` only (never e-mails anything), its own spreadsheet created on first use. Actions: `track` (cookieless daily counters, same pattern as octave-checkout `Track.js`; hits from crawlers, `navigator.webdriver`, headless browsers and our tests go to a separate `Exclus` tab by reason, prerendered pages count only once opened; a hit without the snippet version `v`, i.e. from an old mesure.js still cached by a crawler, is excluded as `legacy`; the snippet URL carries a content hash), `stats` (token: one day's counted vs excluded views and all counter rows), `signup` (interest form and pre-registration, consent text stored), `unsubscribe`, `admin` (token in the git-ignored `gas/src/Secret.js`).


**Bursts (2026-10-18).** On 13/10, before the filter went live at about 11:11 Paris, 936 tender pages were counted in one morning: all `/ao/*`, `(direct)`, 74 % mobile, no filter, search or click, 2 cro.js pages. That is the signature of a JS-rendering crawler, and from 11:11 the same pattern went to `Exclus` as `bot` (289 more that day, 513 on 14/10). Those two Stats rows (`/ao/*` `view` and `tender_view`, `(direct)`, 936 each) were moved to `Exclus` as `burst` with `reclass` and noted in the `Annotations` tab. Defence in depth for crawlers that hide who they are: mesure.js v3 sends `fp`, an 8-hex FNV hash of the user agent, language, screen size and the current hour (it changes every hour). The backend uses it only as a CacheService key (≤ 1 h, never written to the sheet, no IP). More than 8 distinct tender pages in one minute, or more than 30 in one hour with no `filter_use` / `search_use` / `official_click` / `cta_click`, makes the window a `burst`: its hits go to `Exclus`, and the hits of that window already counted are moved there too. Without `fp` (an old cached snippet) only the minute rule applies, on a coarse device + source key. mesure.js v3 also flags a `Headless` client-hints brand, an empty `navigator.languages` and more tools and crawlers (`facebookexternalhit`, `meta-externalagent`, `python`, `curl`, `wget`, `cypress`…). Checked by `tests/burst_check.mjs` (fake Apps Script).
Behaviour analytics and A/B framework (Octave CRO): `static/cro.js` and `gas/src/Cro.js` are vendored byte-identical from `octave-checkout` (`gas/web/jalonel/cro.js`, the Jalonel build with no default host, and `gas/src/Cro.js`; never edit the copies; `tests/cro.check.mjs` is octave-checkout's `gas/web/cro.check.mjs` adapted to that build). `cro.js` is loaded on every page (`data-site="veille-ao-afrique"`, `data-hosts="ao-afrique.jalonel.com"`, `data-cta="[data-mesure],#alerte-form [type=submit]"`) and sends one aggregate batch per page (`track` with `kind:"cro"`): scroll depth, sections reached (`id` / `data-section`), time to first CTA click, forms started/submitted and the name of the last field of an abandoned form, outbound domains, error hashes, Core Web Vitals, rage clicks; daily counters in the `Comportement` and `Experiences` tabs. Goals `signup` and `preorder` fire on a successful sign-up / pre-registration (`static/alertes.js`). One A/B test runs on tender pages, **AOAF-ALERT-INLINE-01** (rule: `octave-asset-ventures/experiments/AOAF-ALERT-INLINE-01.md`): `static/xp.js` (loaded without `defer` before cro.js on `/ao/*` pages only, since the CSP forbids inline scripts) declares `OCTAVE_XP` `aoaf-alert` (a/b, per tab, `?xp_aoaf=a|b` forces an arm) and puts the arm on `<html>` before the first paint, in the same session key cro.js reads. A = current page; B adds `#alerte-rapide` (one e-mail field, the tender's sector and region as hidden fields, consent sentence under the button) right above the official link, shown by CSS only in arm b, and a one-line reminder in that box when the visitor comes back from the official notice. Backend: `CRO_EXPERIMENTS_ = {'aoaf-alert': ['a','b']}`, goals `alert_view`, `official`, `nudge` (+ built-in `signup`); an alert sign-up made on a tender page carries the arm and the form, kept in the Stats detail `aoaf-alert-<a|b>-<inline|rapide>` (unless `test`). Sign-ups sent with `octave_notrack=1` set carry `test: true`. Tender pages are collapsed to `/ao/*` like `Stats` (`croPage_`). Extra actions: `cro` (summary, key whose SHA-256 is in the Script Property `CRO_KEY_SHA256`), `cro_init` (POST, registers that key once); `stats` also returns `cro` for the day.

### E-mail alerts (`gas/src/Alerts.js`, `aoafrique/alerts.py`, `.github/workflows/alerts.yml`; sender since 2026-10-22)

Once a day each active alert sign-up (kind `alertes`, not `test`, not withdrawn; one per address, criteria of its latest row) gets the notices of the public `data/alertes.json` (open notices first seen in the last 8 days) first seen since its last e-mail that match its sectors, regions and zone (`maroc` → portal notices, `afrique-francophone` → World Bank, `benin` / `tunisie` → national portals). Nothing is sent when there is nothing new; `hebdomadaire` gets at most one e-mail every 7 days; 25 notices listed at most, then a link to the list.

**Sender: `alertes@jalonel.com`, never a Google account.** The mailbox is the one MX Plan account included free with the free « Hébergement 100M » attached to jalonel.com (OVHcloud order 261421752, 0 €, zone left unchanged). The address `contact@jalonel.com` stays a redirection. Flow:

1. `alerts.yml` runs at 07:15 UTC (backup 08:50 UTC) and calls `python -m aoafrique alerts --mode real`, which first reads the public `data/alertes.json`.
2. `alerts_plan` (POST, token `ALERTS_TOKEN_` in the git-ignored `Secret.js` = GitHub secret `AOAF_ALERTS_TOKEN`) returns the e-mails of the day (subject, text, HTML, unsubscribe URL); without the token, nothing.
3. `aoafrique/alerts.py` sends them over SMTP (`smtp.mail.ovh.net:465`, SSL, password in GitHub secret `AOAF_SMTP_PASSWORD`) as « Veille AO Afrique (Jalonel) » <alertes@jalonel.com>, reply-to contact@jalonel.com, `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click; `doPost` accepts the form body). It refuses any sender outside @jalonel.com, and the public run logs show neither addresses nor domains.
4. `alerts_confirm` (POST, token) writes `Alertes_etat` (last e-mail per address; only active sign-ups of that mode) and `Alertes_envois` (one row per run, masked addresses only), and for a real run `ALERTS_LAST_RUN`, shown by the public `?action=alerts_status` (`{at, ok, sent, errors, feed_updated}`, `sender`, no personal data) that the daily monitors read.

The Apps Script has no `MailApp`, no `send_mail` scope and no trigger (`sendAlerts`, `installAlerts` and `previewAlerts` are gone; scope `spreadsheets` only: the workflow fetches the public feed and passes it to `alerts_plan`). Scheduled runs send only while the repository variable `ALERTS_LIVE` is `1`, and at most one real run a day (Paris date of `alerts_status`; `workflow_dispatch` input `force=1` overrides). `workflow_dispatch` with `mode=test` mails only the `test` sign-up whose address is the secret `AOAF_TEST_TO`. A failed address is logged and retried the next day; any error fails the run (GitHub notifies) and makes `alerts_status.alerts.ok` false. `python -m aoafrique alerts --mode real --dry-run` (with `AOAF_ALERTS_TOKEN`) is a dry run: masked addresses, nothing sent or written. Checked by `tests/alerts_check.mjs` (fake Apps Script, plan → send → confirm) and `tests/test_alerts.py` (headers, sender guard).

DNS of jalonel.com for this mailbox: MX `mx1/mx2/mx3.mail.ovh.net`, SPF `v=spf1 include:mx.ovh.com -all`, DKIM enabled (CNAME `ovhmo-selector-1/2._domainkey` → OVHcloud, active selector 1), DMARC `v=DMARC1; p=none; adkim=r; aspf=r`. Test of 2026-10-22: spf, dkim and dmarc pass at the receiving server. Creating the MX Plan replaced the old redirect-only e-mail domain: `contact@jalonel.com` → the Octave inbox was recreated identically, and OVHcloud added `postmaster@jalonel.com` → the account's contact address. Secrets live locally in `~/.config/octave/jalonel-mail.env` and `~/.config/octave/aoaf-alerts.env` (mode 600), set with `gh secret set NAME < file`-style reads, never printed.

## Automation

National portals (Benin, Tunisia) are read in the same `update` run as one list each, with the same per-source status and guard (`SOURCES`, `NATIONAL_SOURCES` in `__main__.py`); on the first successful read of a source its notices get `first_seen` = publication date, so the feed and IndexNow are not flooded with a backlog. `python -m aoafrique indexnow --paths pays/ pays/tunisie/ …` sends only the given pages.

`.github/workflows/update.yml`: 05:20 and 17:20 UTC (backup crons at :41 every 2 h run only if that refresh is missing), tests → fetch → commit `data/` → build → GitHub Pages → IndexNow.

Failure tolerance (2026-10-21): one source down (e.g. the Moroccan portal timing out from GitHub) no longer fails the run. The site is published with the other source and the last known notices of the failed one, `data/meta.json` keeps per source `ok`, `at` (last success), `failed_at`, `error`, `count`, the run shows a warning, and the gate retries at the next backup slot. The run fails (exit 1, data files untouched, only `meta.json` records `guard`) if every source failed, if the result is empty, if the total drops below 70 % of the previous run, or if one source loses more than half of its notices; after checking the drop is real, rerun with `workflow_dispatch` input `allow_drop=1`. `/statut.json` publishes that state (read by the daily monitor in `vread-revenue-lab`), and the footer of every page shows the last successful read of each source.

## Tests

`tests/` unit tests on trimmed fixtures. `e2e/` Playwright checks on the live site (390 px and 1440 px): fresh data, filters, form posting to the Sheet, tracking counters. They set `octave_notrack=1` (query or localStorage) so their hits land in the excluded counters, never in visits. `tests/mesure_check.mjs` checks the snippet's bot, webdriver, prerender and opt-out rules under Node. `tests/test_cro.py` runs the vendored `tests/cro.check.mjs` on `static/cro.js` and `tests/cro-gas.check.mjs` on `gas/src/Cro.js`, and checks the wiring (tag on every page, routes, `/ao/*` pages).

## Limits

Up to 12 h behind the portal; rectifications between two runs can be missed; sectors are keyword-based; national portals of Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon and others are not covered yet (checked 2026-10-21: AfDB behind a Cloudflare challenge, UNGM terms forbid reuse in another site, UNDP robots.txt `Disallow: /`, Côte d'Ivoire DGMP list not updated since September, Cameroon PRIDESOFT behind a login and armp.cm 13-18 s per 10-notice page, Senegal/Togo/Burkina/Guinea portals unreachable, UEMOA regional portal domain lapsed).
